\title[Universality of the flare energy index]{Is the power-law index of stellar flare energies universal across spectral types?}

% Nomes, afiliações e autor correspondente a confirmar com o autor
\author[1,$\ast$]{A. Author}
\address[1]{\orgdiv{Department}, \orgname{Institution}, \orgaddress{\country{Brazil}}}
\corresp[$\ast$]{Corresponding author. \href{mailto:author@example.org}{author@example.org}}

\abstract{Flare energies of stars are often assumed to follow one power law, as expected from self-organized criticality. We test whether the index is the same for M, K and G dwarfs in the Kepler flare catalogue of Yang and Liu, with \NflaresSample{} flares in \NstarsSample{} dwarfs. Energies are divided by the stellar luminosity, because the completeness limit in erg follows it. We fit a power law with an exponential cutoff and resample stars, not flares, to obtain errors and tests. The indices are \AlphaCutM$\pm$\AlphaCutErrM{} (M), \AlphaCutK$\pm$\AlphaCutErrK{} (K) and \AlphaCutG$\pm$\AlphaCutErrG{} (G), above the value 3/2 of the fractal-diffusive model. By classes the test of equality is inconclusive. In \NbandsTeff{} narrow bands of effective temperature the index runs from \BandMinAlpha{} to \BandMaxAlpha{} and equality is rejected in every variant of the analysis. The index is therefore not strictly universal. Rotation, which also changes with temperature, may confound its dependence on it.}
