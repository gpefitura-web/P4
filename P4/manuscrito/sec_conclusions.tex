\section{Conclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The S-index variability of HD~166620 in its grand minimum is lower than in both earlier cycle minima. This is descriptive, from one star and published intervals.
\item The rolling lag-1 autocorrelation and variance of annual Mount Wilson series, in \NSegments{} segments of \LMin{} to \NSunYears{} years, show no consistent rise. For HD~166620 the test could not be made as designed because its record ends before the transition, and with an AUC of \AUCTwentyFour{} to \AUCForty{} the test would detect only a strong trend. Nothing follows about stars that are about to lose a cycle.
\item A decisive test needs a series that runs through a transition, a power curve at the real segment lengths with a cycle present, and an analysis of the loss of a limit cycle, for which we know of no predicted sign.
\end{enumerate}
