\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cycle loss lowers variance and autocorrelation in three models, not raises them
\item The surrogate test detects the amplitude fall but has little power for true CSD
\item Records of 100 to 200 years with low noise are needed for any early warning
\item An instrumental step in a series shifts the trend statistic as much as a signal
\item HD 166620 varies less in its grand minimum than in cycle minimum 2
\end{itemize}
